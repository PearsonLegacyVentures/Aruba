IBIZA FAQ

What to know before enquiring.

Can I request beauty, yoga or sound healing instead of a massage?

Yes. Select the closest service in the enquiry form and specify your preferred format. We will check whether a suitable provider can accept it.

Can you organise a treatment at our villa or on a yacht?

You can request both. We first confirm the property's permission, practical setup, access and an appropriately qualified practitioner. Yacht appointments require captain approval and a safe treatment area.

Are Ibiza prices and appointment times confirmed on this page?

No. All fees, provider availability, timing and any travel supplements are confirmed through a written quote before booking or payment.

Can I combine massage, beauty and Pilates for a group?

Yes, as a bespoke request. Please share your group size, desired services, location and timing so we can check staffing and provide a coordinated proposal.
